Make your mark

Hold left click to mine. Right click to place a block on the face you're aiming at. Right click a crafting table to use it. Right click with an apple to eat.

On touchscreens, drag to look and use the movement, mining, and building buttons. Landscape gives you more room.

From wood to diamonds

  1. Logs → planks → sticks and a crafting table.
  2. Use your wooden pickaxe to collect stone and coal.
  3. Place a crafting table. Craft a stone pickaxe nearby to mine iron.
  4. An iron pickaxe unlocks gold and diamonds. Look below Y 12 for gold and Y 9 for diamonds.
  5. Craft torches for caves, bricks for building, and glass for a view.

A few small-world rules: ore drops ready-to-use metal, water stays still, and trees keep their shape. Creative gives you unlimited blocks and flight: F toggle, Space up, Shift down.

Worlds save in this browser every 20 seconds, when paused, and when you leave. Your seed always generates the same terrain.
